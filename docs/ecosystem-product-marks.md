Input image: the attached blue glass R is a material, lighting, color and typography reference.
Replace the letter R with one clearly recognizable uppercase Latin C, in the exact same family: broad elegant rounded typographic strokes sculpted from thick polished optical glass, cool periwinkle-blue translucent volume, deep cobalt refractive edges, subtle warm pearl light glints, restrained internal caustics and realistic reflections. The C must have a generous open counter and clear opening on the right, legible as C at 64 pixels. Keep the premium understated minimal 3D look and slightly three-quarter frontal view of the reference.
One single centered letter C, complete and uncropped, fills approximately 78% of a square canvas. No other letters or wordmark, no base, no pedestal, no background halo, no wall, no ground plane, no checkerboard, no watermark. Genuinely transparent RGBA background outside the object and through the open counter, with clean smooth antialiased silhouette. Preserve highlights and glass reflections inside the letter; do not make the foreground washed out. Output a single square PNG.
